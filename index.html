About us

Our story

How we met

Lucca and Fiona have known each other since grammar school, though for years they were little more than familiar faces. It wasn't until junior year of high school that their paths finally crossed in a meaningful way, and from then on the two were inseparable. It was clear from the start that they brought out the best in each other.

Arizona & San Diego

Both Lucca and Fiona ended up at Arizona State University, where their relationship continued to blossom. After graduation, they built a life together in San Diego, spending nearly four years soaking up the sun, the coast, and everything the city had to offer.

The proposal

On January 31, 2026, Lucca proposed to Fiona at Sunset Cliffs, one of San Diego's most breathtaking spots. She said yes.

What's next

These days, Lucca and Fiona are back in San Francisco, closer to family and ready for what comes next. On July 17, 2027, surrounded by the people they love most, they'll make it official.
